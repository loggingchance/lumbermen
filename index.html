Forestry Operations Software

Lumbermen OS

Field-built software for the way forest operations actually work.

The Core Suite

Each application in the Lumbermen OS suite is a bespoke installation tailored to the specific flow of your business. Because no two forestry operations are identical, your apps shouldn't be either.

In addition to automated daily reporting, every module grants the enterprise owner the power to request individualized, custom reports from the proprietary database built over time through consistent field use.

01 Log Logger Scaling & Inventory

The Digital Tally Book.

This is your mobile-first scaling app designed for fast data entry in the yard. It calculates volume instantly using International 1/4", Doyle, and Scribner Decimal C rules.

Customization

Fully adaptable to any scaling operation, moving from paper tallies to a real-time digital inventory.

Owner Insight

Automated reports run on your schedule, plus the ability to request custom logs-received or species-mix reports at any time from your historical scaling data.

02 Saw Logger Sawmill Management

The Mill Operations Suite.

This is the mill operations suite — a digital solution for data collection at the mill, allowing for customized tracking of production and flow.

Customization

Bridges the unique gap between your raw log inventory and your specific physical milling process.

Owner Insight

Automated reports run on your schedule, plus on-demand queries for custom production trends or efficiency snapshots beyond standard summaries.

03 Cut Mark Yield Analysis

The Profitability Engine.

This is your specialized tool for mill studies. It tracks specific log inputs against the finished lumber output to calculate overrun and grade recovery.

Customization

Acts as the "translator" that tells a mill manager exactly how much value they are getting out of every sawlog grade based on their specific equipment.

Owner Insight

Automated reports run on your schedule, plus deep-dive recovery reports to analyze the long-term impact of specific timber sources on your bottom line.

04 Cordr Firewood Management

The Order Flow Tool.

Named to rhyme with "order," this app handles the specific logistics of the firewood business. It manages customer orders and inventory, ensuring processing and delivery stay as organized and on track as the rest of your operation. Visit the Cordr page →

Customization

Built to match your specific delivery zones, moisture requirements, and customer tiers.

Owner Insight

Automated reports run on your schedule, plus custom reports on regional demand or historical customer ordering patterns.

05 PACE Cost & Productivity

The Benchmarking Standard.

PACE — Productivity Analysis and Cost Evaluation — is the benchmarking tool for logging operations. It converts a company's full product mix into a common unit (tons), then calculates cost per ton and tons per machine hour from real overhead, payroll, fuel, and equipment data. The output is a complete cost-by-product report: dollars per MBF for sawlogs, per cord for firewood, per ton for pulpwood and chips.

Customization

Covers cut-to-length, whole-tree, tree-length, and hybrid systems across all major US regions. Conversion factors are adjustable to match local species and market conditions.

Owner Insight

Generates a complete benchmarking report broken down by product and submittable for regional aggregation — giving individual operators a window into where they stand across the supply chain.
